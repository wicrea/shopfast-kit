For the daily Sales dashboard, an **active customer** is someone who **bought something in the last 6 months**.
Our marketing campaigns target active customers, and 6 months matches our seasonal collections.
Also, when I say "net revenue" I mean what the customer paid minus refunds. Please don't remove VAT, the sales team
is paid on amounts including VAT.
